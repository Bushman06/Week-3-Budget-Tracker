How your information is stored

Your email, original sign-up name and sign-in details are managed by Netlify Identity. Your name, phone, currency, privacy preference and signed-in budget records are stored in Netlify Database. Your photo is stored privately in Netlify Blobs and is available only through your signed-in account.

Guest budgets and the appearance preference stay in this browser. Guest records are not automatically added to an account. Hide amounts is a display preference; sign out on shared devices to protect your account.

Remove photo deletes your uploaded image. Clear Profile removes your saved name, phone, photo and profile preferences; your sign-in account and budget records remain.
